## Analyst Commentary (Claude Fable 5)
### The Big Picture
This week is the mirror image of the last two: record spend and the account's best impression share ever, but only 3 leads at a cost per lead more than double target. Part of that drop is honest accounting, since the prior two 10-lead weeks were inflated by duplicate conversions logged on single clicks, and part is a real conversion rate slide, because clicks were nearly identical to last week while leads fell by seven. The owner's July 28 overhaul of roughly 45 ads across every campaign landed mid-week, so this week's numbers mix old and new copy and next week is the first clean read on it. The one won sod job still covered the week's spend by almost five to one, and the 90-day web form pipeline remains healthy, so this looks like a rough week, not a broken account. The structural story is unchanged: three-quarters of eligible auctions are lost to ad rank and none to budget, so quality, not money, is still the lever.

### Anomalies & Watch Items
- BH_CommercialMaint_Search is missing from the spend table, and the owner changed its status July 28 hours after rebuilding it (new negatives, new target CPA, rewritten ads). It appears paused. If that was intentional, fine; the odd part is pausing a campaign right after setting it up.
- The target CPA setting on BH_CommercialMaint_Search was changed twice in 27 hours, first by the web dev team on July 27, then by the owner on July 28. Neither value is in the log. Under the spend freeze, this setting must not have gone up.
- The automated negative list would block "sprinkler service near me" as a search term in the same week the keyword of that name produced one of the three conversions. Same trap as prior weeks.
- Quality score finally moved in the right direction: two keywords rose two points each and "french drain fort worth" hit 10, the account's first perfect score. Likely early payoff from the July 28 ad rewrites.
- 12p-4p and 8p-12a together took $288 with zero conversions this week, but those same blocks carried 8 of 10 conversions last week. The pattern flips weekly, so no schedule change is justified.
- The irrigation promo posted its second straight week above the pre-promo baseline on CTR. It still trails the other campaigns, but the trend says keep it running.

No typos visible. Exact match is fine for brand names, though the low-intent terms would block more variants as phrase match.

- Five assets were created July 27 with no text logged. Given this team's history of typo'd text and broken tracking URL syntax, these need a read-through and a live click test.
- No delete-then-recreate of live keywords this week, so no performance history damage detected.
