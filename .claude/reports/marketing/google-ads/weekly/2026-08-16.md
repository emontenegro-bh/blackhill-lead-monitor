## Analyst Commentary (Claude Fable 5)
### The Big Picture
On paper this is the worst tracked week yet: one form lead at a cost more than four times target, and impression share slid for the third straight week, all of it lost to ad rank and none to budget. The revenue picture tells a different story: seven won deals traced back to ads, worth roughly ten times the week's spend, so the ads are still bringing in real customers. Part of that gap is by design, since phone call leads are deliberately not counted in the primary number, but the pattern this week is odd in a specific way: CTR hit its best level in the dataset while almost nobody who clicked filled out the form. That points to something breaking after the click, not before it, and the web dev team rewrote four ads including their tracking URLs on August 10, the first day of this reporting week. Until those ads are click-tested, we cannot rule out self-inflicted damage to the form or tracking.

### Anomalies & Watch Items
- Conversion rate collapsed (1 lead from 52 clicks) in the same week CTR rose to 3.9%. Good clicks, no forms. Strongest suspect is a post-click problem tied to the August 10 ad and tracking URL changes. Not proven, needs the test in Action 1.
- Mobile: zero conversions on 39 clicks, and zero on 23 clicks last week. July mobile converted heavily, so this may be variance, but two straight zero weeks plus the tracking URL changes make a mobile form test mandatory before any bid change.
- Impression share has fallen three straight weeks (24% to 18% to 16%) with spend frozen and zero lost to budget. Competitors are outranking us, and "sprinkler repair weatherford tx" dropped two quality score points this week. The quality problem is getting worse, not better.
- The headline "Fort Worth Sprinkler Repair" is back with 272 impressions at 2.6% CTR after multiple prior recommendations to remove it. It likely came back in the August 10 rewrite.
- "irrigation system installation" paid $27.50 for a single click. One data point, but that is an extremely expensive click worth checking for a runaway bid on that keyword.
- "sod installation aledo tx" vanished from the quality score tracker with no keyword change in the log. Probably just zero impressions this week, but the data cannot confirm it was not paused.
- Irrigation promo CTR recovered to 3.20%, back above the pre-promo baseline after last week's dip. Keep the promo running.
- BH_CommercialMaint_Search remains absent from the account. The owner has still not confirmed whether the July 28 pause was deliberate.

### Web Dev Team Change Review
- August 10, 4:06 to 4:23 AM: four responsive ads in BH_PC_Irrigationservice were updated, each changing headlines, descriptions, the tracking URL template, and the final URL suffix. The log does not show the new text or URLs.
- Tracking URL templates and URL suffixes are the exact fields this team has broken before. With four ads changed at once and the week's conversion rate collapsing immediately after, every one of these ads needs a live click test. This is Action 1.
- The new headline and description text is not in the log, so I cannot check for typos. The weak "Fort Worth Sprinkler Repair" headline reappearing this week suggests the rewrite may have re-added copy we removed on purpose. The team should send the full text of all four ads.
- No keyword changes, no negatives touched, and no delete-then-recreate of keywords this week, so no performance history damage detected.
