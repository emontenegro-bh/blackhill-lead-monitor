## Analyst Commentary (Claude Fable 5)
### The Big Picture

This week gave back most of last week's gains: spend halved, leads halved, and impression share slid from roughly a quarter of eligible auctions back toward a fifth, all of it lost to ad rank and none to budget. The one bright spot is that all three ad-attributed wins were irrigation assessments, so the irrigation repair terms remain the account's proven revenue source and ads again more than paid for themselves in closed work. The prime suspect for the slide is timing: the web dev team rewrote the main irrigation ad and its tracking URLs on August 3, and the irrigation campaign's clickthrough rate promptly fell back below the pre-promo baseline after two weeks above it. Until we see the text of that rewrite and the roughly 35 new keywords loaded August 4, we cannot separate a demand dip from self-inflicted damage. The lever is unchanged: quality and copy, not money.

### Anomalies & Watch Items

- Irrigation campaign clickthrough fell to less than half the control campaigns and back below the pre-promo baseline the same week the web dev team rewrote its ad. That correlation is strong enough to investigate but not enough to state as fact; the promo read is now muddied either way.
- Spend and clicks halved with an empty budget log and zero impressions lost to budget. Either search demand cooled or the August 3 to 4 changes hurt our ad rank. Data is insufficient to say which yet.
- Mobile took nearly half the spend with zero conversions, but mobile carried most of the account's leads in mid-July. The pattern flips weekly, so no device bid change is justified.
- "landscape design aledo tx" is now the number two spend keyword with zero conversions and Low ad relevance, meaning the ads it triggers do not use its wording. A watch, and a copy fix candidate if it repeats.
- The automated negative list again includes core repair terms like "irrigation repair near me" and "sprinkler service near me", both of which have converted in prior weeks. Do not apply the list blindly. One term has, however, now earned its negative; see Priority Actions.
- Minor bookkeeping: this report shows last week's spend as $602 but last week's report said $583. Small, but the two sources disagree again.
- BH_CommercialMaint_Search remains absent from the spend table. The owner's answer on whether the July 28 pause was deliberate is still outstanding.

This team has previously shipped broken ValueTrack URL syntax, and the log does not show the new values. A live click test is required, and the new headline text needs a read-through since the campaign's clickthrough dropped immediately after this change.
- August 4: roughly 30 new campaign criteria were created in BH_PC_Irrigationservice and 5 in BH_LeadGen_Form_Search. The keyword text and match types are not in the log, so I cannot check for typos (like the past "irrigation istallation") or inconsistent match types. This review is incomplete without the list.

If the removed keyword was recreated with the same text, that is a delete-then-recreate, which resets the keyword's performance history and is real damage. The log does not show the text, so this needs an answer from the team.
- Four assets were created August 4 with no text logged. Given past typo issues, they need a read-through in the account.
