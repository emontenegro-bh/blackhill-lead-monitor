## Analyst Commentary (Claude Fable 5)
### The Big Picture
This is the second straight week under the cost-per-lead target, and the real news is where the leads came from: the form campaign, dead for four of the last five weeks, produced four leads at $33 each right after the owner's September 8 keyword rebuild, with the brand-new "landscaping services" keyword converting three times within days. The irrigation campaign went the other way, landing over target for the first time in three weeks, right as its ad group cost targets were edited twice in two days. The chronic problem has not moved: we still appear in about one of six eligible searches, every lost auction is lost to ad rank and none to budget, and quality score slipped this week instead of improving. On top of that, the automated page checker could not reach eight landing pages covering nearly every ad group, which must be resolved before anything else because it either means the checker broke or the site did.

### Anomalies & Watch Items
- All 8 flagged landing pages failed with the identical error "Network is unreachable." That pattern usually means the checking tool lost its own connection, not that eight pages died at once, and the week's six conversions argue the site was working at least part of the week. But the data cannot confirm the pages are fine, so a manual load test is mandatory (Action 1).
- Quality score is moving the wrong way: average fell from 6.1 to 5.5 and the count of keywords below 5 nearly doubled to 7 of 25. This is the engine behind the 83% of auctions lost to rank.
- Irrigation campaign conversions dropped 60% and its cost per lead doubled past target the same week its ad group target CPAs were changed on Sep 9 (web dev) and again Sep 10 (owner). One week is not proof, but the timing overlaps.
- The form campaign's impression share fell by a third while its spend more than doubled. Reading between the lines: the Sep 8 keyword additions widened the pool of searches it is eligible for, so it is spending more while covering a smaller slice of a bigger pool. Not damage, but it means that 17% is not comparable to prior weeks.
- New keyword "retaining wall" spent $20 with no leads in its first days. Too new to cut, but it has no history to lean on.
- Bookkeeping: this report shows last week's CPA as $71 and impression share as 18%, but last week's report said $61 and 17%. The sources disagree again.

### Web Dev Team Change Review
- Sep 9, 7:20 AM: three ad group target CPA changes in BH_PC_Irrigationservice with no values logged. The owner edited the same three fields the next day, which may have been a correction. Both sets of values are needed in writing, because the irrigation campaign's cost per lead doubled this week and a raised target would also conflict with the spend freeze.
- Sep 7, morning and midday: ten keyword status flips in BH_LeadGen_Form_Search with no indication of pause versus enable. That campaign then produced four leads, so damage looks unlikely, but the list of which keywords and which direction is still needed.
- Twelve assets created Sep 7-9 with no text logged. Given this team's typo history, each needs a read-through in the account before next report.
- The good news: no keyword creates, no tracking URL or final URL changes, and no delete-then-recreate patterns from this team this week. None of their known breakage patterns are present in what the log shows; the open items are unlogged values, not visible errors.

### Priority Actions
The binding constraint is unchanged: 83% of eligible auctions in both campaigns lost to ad rank with zero lost to budget, and it worsened this week because quality score fell. The most fixable quality driver right now is the unresolved landing page question, since landing page experience is a component of ad rank. If quality fixes plateau with impression share still under 20%, the remaining gap can only be closed with more spend, and that decision belongs to the owner, not this report.

1. If any page fails to load, point that ad group's final URLs at the closest working service page the same day and have the web dev team restore the broken page. If all eight load, the checker failed, not the site, and no account change is needed. Payoff: a dead page wastes every click sent to it and drags landing page experience, which feeds the ad rank problem directly. Confidence: high
2. Get the three Sep 9 target CPA values for the BH_PC_Irrigationservice ad groups from the web dev team in writing today, plus the owner's Sep 10 values, and cut any target above $80 down to $80. That is a cut, so it fits the spend freeze. Payoff: the likeliest explanation for the irrigation campaign's cost per lead doubling this week, and fixing it brings that campaign back under target. Confidence: medium
3. In the BH_PC_Irrigationservice ad group containing "lawn sprinkler repair near me" (quality score 3 for six straight reports), add the headline "Lawn Sprinkler Repair Experts" so the ad uses the keyword's own words, pin "Veteran-Owned, Locally Trusted" to position 1, and remove "Fort Worth Sprinkler Repair" if it is still live. Payoff: lifts expected clickthrough and ad relevance, which wins back a share of the auctions lost to rank at the same frozen budget. Confidence: high
4. Get two lists in writing from the web dev team by Friday: which ten keywords in BH_LeadGen_Form_Search had status flipped on Sep 7 and whether each was paused or enabled, and the full text of the twelve assets created Sep 7-9, checked for typos before they run another week. Payoff: the form campaign just became the week's lead engine at $33 per lead, and this protects it from silent changes and typo'd copy. Confidence: high
