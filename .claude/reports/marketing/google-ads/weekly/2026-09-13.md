### The Big Picture
This is the third straight week under the $80 cost-per-lead target, and the story flipped between campaigns: the form campaign, dead for four of the last five weeks, produced four leads at $33 each right after the September 7-8 keyword work, while the irrigation campaign that carried all summer dropped to two leads at $106, above target for the first time in a month. Total leads at six are still barely half the goal, and the reason has not changed all summer: we show in about one of six eligible searches, and every single lost auction is lost to ad rank, none to budget. Average quality score slipped from 6.1 to 5.4 this week with seven keywords now below 5, so the rank problem is drifting the wrong way. Closed ad revenue was also the thinnest of the dataset, roughly a wash against spend, though one week of closed deals proves little. With spend frozen, quality score and ad copy remain the only levers, and if those plateau with impression share still under 20 percent, the remaining gap can only be closed with more spend, which is the owner's call, not this report's.
