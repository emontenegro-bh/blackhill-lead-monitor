## Analyst Commentary (Claude Fable 5)
### The Big Picture
This was the second-best lead week on record and the second straight week under the $80 cost-per-lead target, with the irrigation campaign producing all five leads at $50 each and eight ad-sourced jobs closing for roughly fourteen times the week's spend. The irrigation promo also posted its strongest combined read yet, with clickthrough well above the pre-promo baseline and a conversion rate more than four times it. The problem is the same one it has been all summer: we show up in barely one of six eligible searches, every lost auction is lost to ad rank and none to budget, so competitors are still taking most of the leads that exist. Meanwhile the form campaign went zero for the fourth time in five weeks, and the web dev team rebuilt that exact campaign's keywords, negatives, and cost targets on the first morning of this reporting week, so we cannot yet separate weak demand from the rebuild. Efficiency is fixed; volume is the whole fight, and with spend frozen the only lever is quality.

### Anomalies & Watch Items
- One click on "lawn care services near me" cost $44.61, about 15 percent of the week's budget. It did convert, so it is not waste, but that price points to a runaway bid or a broad match reaching an expensive auction. Worth a bid cap, see Priority Actions.
- BH_LeadGen_Form_Search spend halved and conversions hit zero the same morning (Aug 31) the web dev team rewrote its keyword list, added roughly ten campaign-level criteria, flipped the status on eleven keywords, and changed two ad group cost targets. One week is not proof of damage, but the timing means next week's read on this campaign is really a read on their rebuild.
- The search term "sprinkler repair fort worth" finally converted this week after five straight reports recommending it as a negative. The negative was never added, and this week that turned out to be lucky. That recommendation is now retired; do not add it.
- Several keywords vanished from the quality score tracker versus last week, including "irrigation installers" and "sprinkler system installation". This may be the Aug 31 removals or just zero impressions; the data cannot say which.
- Midday (12p-4p) took a quarter of spend with zero leads while evening and night carried four of five. Prior weeks show midday leads, so the data is too thin to justify a schedule cut yet.
- Bookkeeping: this report states last week as 8 conversions, $479 spend, 19% impression share; last week's report said 7, $412, 18%. Likely a late-recorded conversion, but the sources disagree again.

- One likely case of real damage: at 3:53:59 AM a keyword was removed (the removal record includes a final URL field, meaning it was an established keyword) and exact match "[landscaping companies in my area]" was created in the same second. - Two ad group target CPA changes at 3:56 AM in BH_LeadGen_Form_Search with no values logged. If either was raised, that conflicts with the spend freeze; values needed in writing.
- Roughly ten campaign-level criteria (probably negatives) were added 4:01 to 4:09 AM with text not visible, including two remove-and-recreate pairs. A prior web dev negative in this campaign nearly blocked a converting term, so the list needs to be seen.
- Eleven keyword status flips at 6:19 to 6:20 AM with no indication of pause versus enable. Unknown which keywords.
- Aug 31, 5:27 PM: a responsive ad in BH_PC_Irrigationservice was rewritten including the tracking URL template and final URL suffix, the two fields this team has broken before. Sep 1: a keyword-level final URL was also changed in the same campaign. Both need a live click test.
- Eight assets created Aug 31 and Sep 1 with no text logged; they need a read-through in the account given past typo history.
